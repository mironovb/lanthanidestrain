# Audit of the 29 July re-analysis: one invalid headline of mine, three overstatements

**Bogdan Mironov · 30 July 2026**

A deliberate adversarial pass over yesterday's nine measurements. It found one
result of mine that must be **withdrawn**, three that must be **softened**, and
five that survive unchanged.

---

## What the metric is

Every number in this study is `sel_adj_logSF_r2`, from
[`adjacent_pair_metrics`](../evaluation.py) via `adjacent_pair_arrays`:

1. Group rows by **`composition_key`** = extractant ‖ *binned* condition columns.
2. Within a block, average **`y` and `p`** per lanthanide → one point per
   (block, metal).
3. Keep pairs with `|Δ lanthanide_index| == 1`. Pm is absent from the data, so
   index 5 never occurs and Nd/Sm is correctly *not* adjacent.
4. `dy = y_i − y_j`, `dp = p_i − p_j`, `i` the lighter lanthanide.
5. `R² = 1 − Σ(dy−dp)² / Σ(dy − mean(dy))²`.

**905 pairs, 552 blocks, 4,746 rows, 162 extractants.**

Two properties that govern every interpretation, and neither was stated plainly
before:

- **`mean(dy) = −0.0724`** (SD 0.2707). The null model is "every pair separates by
  −0.072", so **the direction of the lanthanide trend is free**. R² > 0 means
  beating the series-average trend — i.e. predicting *ligand-specific* selectivity,
  not the contraction.
- **Both sides are averaged in step 2.** A model that predicts every *row* exactly
  scores R² = 1. This is the fact that kills the ceiling below.

## E1 — MAJOR. The +0.679 ceiling is invalid. Withdrawn.

`ceiling_test.py`'s only surviving estimator (E2) measured the SD of the pair
difference **across distinct exact condition sets inside one binned block** and
treated it as irreducible noise. It is not irreducible, for two independent
reasons:

1. **The model can predict it.** The `cond` block is **64 exact numeric columns**
   and is part of `baseline_2d`. **254 of 552** binned blocks (46 %) have at least
   one `cond__` feature varying inside them, so the model can tell those rows
   apart.
2. **The metric averages it away on both sides.** Per-condition variation never
   enters the binned metric at all.

**The contradiction that proves it.** Within the 203-pair subset E2 was measured
on, the implied ceiling is **+0.173** — *below* the best model's +0.267. Nothing
can exceed a real ceiling. The +0.679 came from dividing that subset's noise
variance (0.0235) by the **full** 905-pair set's variance (0.0733); the two
populations differ 2.6-fold in spread, and the ratio mixes them.

| propagation | ceiling |
|---|---|
| flat, as shipped | **+0.6793** |
| divided by n_conditions | +0.7393 |
| **measured within the subset it came from** | **+0.1727** |

All three estimators fail: **E1 and E3** on a non-representative subset (a cell
acquires duplicates precisely when two sources disagree), **E2** on measuring a
predictable quantity rather than noise.

### Withdrawn

- "Ceiling on adjacent-pair R² ≤ +0.679"
- "the best model reaches **39 %** of what this dataset allows"
- "**+0.412** of headroom"
- and every statement resting on them, including *"the problem is
  measurement-limited and by how much is now known"* and the framing of the four
  closed routes as *"routes to the headroom"*.

The four closure results themselves stand — recombination, energetics, conformers
and the objective each failed on its own terms. What is withdrawn is the claim that
we know **how much** room those routes were failing to reach.

### What replaces it

The right quantity is the disagreement between rows whose **feature vectors are
identical** — same extractant, same metal, same `cond__` to 6 dp, therefore the
same ECFP, RDKit block, complex and geometry. That disagreement is irreducible for
any model, and it is what E1 was reaching for before representativeness defeated
it.

`raw_data/*_SAFE.csv` carries a **`DOI`** column that the ML table dropped.
Joining it back splits that disagreement into **inter-laboratory source conflict**
and **intra-paper replication** — which is exactly the distinction that made E1
unusable. `ceiling_v2.py` does this; if the ceiling remains unidentifiable
afterwards, that is itself a publishable statement about the dataset.

## E2 — MODERATE. Latent bug in the conformer gap.

`mtd_ensemble.py` records `relaxed[0]` as the shipped geometry's relaxed energy.
But `relaxed` receives an entry **only when an optimisation succeeds**, and the
shipped geometry is merely `picks[0]`. **6 of 120** complexes returned fewer than
17 successful relaxations, so for those `relaxed[0]` may be a metadynamics snapshot
and the reported `gap` is meaningless.

The published verdict is a median over 9 families and is very likely unaffected,
but the code is wrong. The fix is to carry the shipped structure's index explicitly
instead of relying on position 0, and to record `NaN` when its own optimisation
failed. `conformer_diagnostic.csv` is recomputed and `CONFORMER_RESULTS.md`
amended.

## E3 — MODERATE. The objective main effect was overstated.

`OBJECTIVE_RESULTS.md` says the `level_weight` effect is "monotone in the wrong
direction" and "more weight on the block mean is better", as established fact.

| `level_weight` | mean tune adj R² |
|---|---|
| 0.1 | +0.2286 |
| 0.3 | +0.2351 |
| 1.0 | +0.2412 |

Span **0.0126**, against an SE of the difference of **~0.0092** derived from the
measured 8-seed ensemble spread — **1.4 σ**. A monotone ordering across three
levels at 1.4 σ is *suggestive*, not established, and this study has a documented
history of 2.9 σ claims collapsing to 0.1 σ on replication.

**The conclusion survives**, because it does not rest on the main effect: the
confirmatory contrast OBJ − S0 = **−0.0233 [−0.0325, −0.0044]** is a separate and
better-powered test. Only the mechanism sentence needs softening.

## E4 — MINOR. "Recombination is exhausted" is too strong.

`full_stack` nests the **arm selection** — for each held-out extractant the arm
set, order and weights are all chosen on the other 161. The published three-arm
stack nests only the *weights*; its arm set was chosen by a human who had already
seen the results.

So **+0.2604 vs +0.2672** is partly selection bias in favour of the published
number, not evidence that no better combination exists. The defensible claim is:
*greedy nested forward selection over 15 arms does not beat a hand-chosen 3-arm
stack.*

---

## E5 — MINOR. The D0 capacity sentence is wrong — in my conclusion's favour.

`ENCODER_RESULTS.md` says the arms share "the same embedding width … so the head
sees an identically shaped vector and no capacity difference is smuggled in."

`DistanceNet` holds **2 of its 9 readout blocks at exactly zero** (the slots
`SimplicialNet` fills from the triangle level). The *width* matches; the
*information* does not — D0 carries less. That makes D0 matching S0 a
**conservative** comparison, which strengthens the conclusion, but the sentence as
written is inaccurate and is corrected.

---

## Also: the look-count bookkeeping is arbitrary

`N_LOOKS` was assigned per module in **authoring** order — 10, 13, 16, 19, 20 —
not execution order. `energy_test` ran before the encoder arrays finished yet
claims 16 against the encoder's 13. Bonferroni with too many looks is
conservative, so no interval is too narrow, but the accounting should read
"**≥ N looks**" rather than implying an exact ledger.

## The honest state of the claims after this audit

1. **A learned 3D representation adds adjacent-lanthanide selectivity that 2D
   fingerprints lack** — ~+0.03 under the *binned* metric, not distinguishable from
   zero under the *strict* one.
2. **The representation family is broad, not narrow.** Simplicial, graph-only and
   distance encoders are mutually indistinguishable; fixed persistence images lose.
   The transferable object is the accurate-and-decorrelated **rule**, not the
   complex.
3. **Four routes failed on their own terms** — recombination, energetics,
   conformers, objective. Each is a real negative.
4. **How much room those routes were failing to reach is now unknown.** That was
   the ceiling's job and the ceiling was wrong.

Point 4 is the reason the next step is `ceiling_v2` rather than another model: it
is not worth optimising toward an unmeasured target.

## Appendix — what the DOI join actually found, including two wrong guesses of mine

The ceiling rescue rests on joining `raw_data/*_SAFE.csv` back to the modelled
rows. **The join works**: `safe_exp_id` is `<file stem>:<exp_id>`, and keying on
`(file, exp_id)` matches **100 %** of 4,746 rows across **110 DOIs**.

### The disagreement is *within* papers, not between them

Of the 273 cells that share a `strict_composition_key` and a metal — i.e. have
identical model feature vectors — and still disagree on log D:

| | cells | pooled SD | median SD |
|---|---|---|---|
| **within one DOI** | **257 (94 %)** | **0.7295** | 0.2384 |
| across DOIs | 16 (6 %) | 0.2434 | 0.0000 |

So the scatter that defeated E1 is **not inter-laboratory source conflict**. That
was my hypothesis and it is wrong. The dataset's curation is not the problem.

### A quarter of it is a recorded covariate the pipeline drops

Of those 257 within-paper cells, **25 % have a recorded-but-dropped column varying
inside them**, and those cells are noisier than the rest:

| | cells | pooled SD | median SD |
|---|---|---|---|
| a dropped covariate varies | 63 | **0.9755** | 0.3041 |
| nothing recorded varies | 194 | 0.6470 | 0.2312 |

The varying columns are `ini_comp` (57 cells), `Solvent_Name` (35) and
`Phase_Modifier_Concentration_M` (6).

**And `ini_comp` turns out to be the same finding as `Solvent_Name`, not a second
one.** It is a composed string — *"HNO3, HOAmBenDGA, Eu, n-dodecane with 5 vol%
iso-decanol"* — carrying acid, extractant, metal and **diluent**. Its samples are
all single-metal, so my "single-feed versus mixed-feed" reading of it was also
wrong. When `ini_comp` varies inside one of these cells, what actually varies is
the **diluent**.

### The one real, cheap data-quality lever

`cond__diluent__other` is a single one-hot column holding **42 distinct raw
solvents**, covering **261 of 4,746 rows (5.5 %)**. Those rows are mutually
indistinguishable to the model although the experiments differ, which is exactly
how they show up as irreducible-looking label noise.

That is a genuine information loss and a cheap fix. It is also **modest** — 5.5 %
of rows — and should not be inflated into a headline.

### Two guesses of mine that were simply wrong

Worth recording because they were plausible and would have been embarrassing to
propose:

- **`Holdback_Agent_Name` / `_Concentration_M`** — a masking agent is precisely a
  selectivity modifier, so I expected this to matter. It is **0 % populated** in
  the modelled rows.
- **Phase ratio (`volValue`)** — 57 % populated but **constant** where present.
  Useless.

Also empty: `Acid_Concentration_Organic_M`, `Radiolytic_Dosage_kGy`,
`thirdType`/`thirdValue`. `Shaking_Time_min` is 18.9 % populated over 11 values —
too sparse to use.

### Consequence for the ceiling

The quantity E1 wanted — disagreement between rows with genuinely identical
experimental conditions — **is still not identifiable**, because 75 % of the
disagreeing cells have nothing recorded varying and their median SD is 0.231 log
units, which propagated to a pair of singleton cells exceeds the entire observed
spread of the target. The subset that could measure it is 6.6 % of cells and
selected non-randomly.

**So: the ceiling stays withdrawn, and the reason is now specific** — it is not
that the data is dirty, and not that papers disagree with each other. It is that
within a single paper, at conditions the pipeline records as identical, log D
varies by ~0.23 log units and the feature set cannot say why for three-quarters of
those cases.
