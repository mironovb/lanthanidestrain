## Protocol

- Target `log_D`; split = leave-extractants-out, grouped K-fold on `extractant_group` (canonical extractant SMILES, 190 groups).
- Every reported number is computed from **out-of-fold** predictions, so no extractant is ever in train and test at the same time.
- `R2_between` = R^2 of the per-extractant mean (size weighted): can the model rank whole extractants?
- `R2_within`  = R^2 after removing each extractant's own mean: can it reproduce the spread *inside* one extractant (metal + conditions)?
- `R2_within_composition` removes extractant **and** condition set, leaving pure lanthanide-series selectivity.
- `sel_*` metrics are computed inside composition blocks with >= 3 lanthanides: Spearman of the predicted series order, R^2 of pairwise log separation factors, and the sign accuracy of those pairs.
